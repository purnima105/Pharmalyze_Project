💊 Pharmalyze
📌 Overview
Pharmalyze is a web-based platform designed to streamline pharmacy inventory management and enhance collaboration between pharmacies and medical suppliers. It acts as an all-in-one solution by integrating inventory tracking, report generation, and eCommerce functionality into a single, user-friendly system.

The platform helps pharmacies maintain accurate stock levels, reduce manual errors, and improve operational efficiency, while also enabling seamless supplier interaction and online ordering.

🚀 Key Features
📦 Inventory Management
Real-time tracking of medicines and medical products
Automatic stock updates on purchases and sales
Low-stock alerts to prevent shortages
Batch-wise and expiry date management

📊 Reporting & Analytics
Detailed inventory reports
Sales and purchase summaries
Stock movement analysis
Downloadable reports for auditing and record-keeping

🛒 eCommerce Integration
Online product listing for suppliers
Direct ordering system for pharmacies
Order status tracking and order history
Improved transparency between pharmacies and suppliers

🔐 Secure & User-Friendly
Role-based access control (Admin, Pharmacy, Supplier)
Secure authentication and authorization
Simple and intuitive user interface

🛠️ Technology Stack
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: PHP
Database: MySQL
Server: Apache (XAMPP)

🎯 Project Objective
Pharmalyze aims to digitize and simplify pharmacy operations by providing a centralized system for inventory control, supplier communication, and online ordering. The platform improves accuracy, efficiency, and decision-making through real-time data and detailed reports.
